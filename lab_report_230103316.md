## Phase 1 — False Sharing and Karp–Flatt Analysis

The total deterministic update workload was M = 24,000,000. The stride test used eight processes, two warm-up runs and seven timed runs.

### Table 1A — Stride sensitivity (p=8)

| Stride (64-bit words) | Byte separation | Median (ms) | IQR (ms) | Throughput (Mops/s) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 8 | 700.65 | 47.42 | 34.254 |
| 2 | 16 | 705.48 | 43.13 | 34.020 |
| 4 | 32 | 734.51 | 18.70 | 32.675 |
| 8 | 64 | 719.18 | 24.12 | 33.371 |
| 16 | 128 | 705.02 | 7.22 | 34.041 |
| 32 | 256 | 783.09 | 123.29 | 30.648 |

**Cache-line boundary conclusion:** The reported cache line is 128 bytes, so stride 16 places consecutive worker slots at least 128 bytes apart. However, the measured times do **not** exhibit a sharp drop at stride 16: stride 1 was 700.65 ms and stride 16 was 705.02 ms. This run therefore does not empirically resolve a false-sharing transition. The variance and Python interpreter overhead may obscure cache-coherence effects; it would be inaccurate to claim a measured 128-byte performance inflection.

### Table 1B — Process scaling and Karp–Flatt

| Stride | p | Median (ms) | Speedup Sp | Efficiency Ep | Karp–Flatt e(p) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 | 3290.39 | 1.000 | 1.000 | N/A |
| 1 | 2 | 1645.38 | 2.000 | 1.000 | 0.0001 |
| 1 | 4 | 930.21 | 3.537 | 0.884 | 0.0436 |
| 1 | 8 | 700.65 | 4.696 | 0.587 | 0.1005 |
| 16 | 1 | 3057.96 | 1.000 | 1.000 | N/A |
| 16 | 2 | 1613.19 | 1.896 | 0.948 | 0.0551 |
| 16 | 4 | 898.47 | 3.404 | 0.851 | 0.0584 |
| 16 | 8 | 705.02 | 4.337 | 0.542 | 0.1206 |

The diagnostic formula for p >= 2 is `e(p) = (1/Sp - 1/p) / (1 - 1/p)`, where `Sp = T1/Tp` and `Ep = Sp/p`. For stride 1, e(p) grew from approximately 0.0001 at p=2 to 0.1005 at p=8. For stride 16, it rose from 0.0551 to 0.1206. Both trajectories show increasing effective overhead as concurrency grows. Contrary to the simple false-sharing hypothesis, stride 16 did not consistently reduce e(p); no causal attribution to cache invalidation is justified from these measurements alone.

## Phase 2 — Strong and Weak Scaling

Strong scaling held N=1,600,000 elements fixed. Weak scaling used N0=250,000 elements per worker, so N(p)=p*N0. The pool was created outside each trial's timed interval.

### Table 2A — Strong scaling

| p | Median Tstrong (ms) | Sp | Ep |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2977.30 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| 2 | 1569.33 | 1.897 | 0.949 |
| 3 | 1087.72 | 2.737 | 0.912 |
| 4 | 860.92 | 3.458 | 0.865 |
| 5 | 818.03 | 3.640 | 0.728 |
| 6 | 778.43 | 3.825 | 0.637 |
| 7 | 735.78 | 4.046 | 0.578 |
| 8 | 712.88 | 4.176 | 0.522 |

The serial fraction was `s = TStageA(1)/Tstrong(1) = 0.014201`. Amdahl's idealized asymptotic maximum is `S∞ = 1/s = 70.420`, and its p=8 prediction is `1/[s+(1-s)/8] = 7.277`, compared with observed `S8 = 4.176`. The large gap is consistent with non-serial hardware and interpreter/communication overheads and heterogeneous core performance; Amdahl's model alone does not account for these costs.

**Core-cluster inflection:** On this Apple M2, no SMT boundary is present (physical = logical = 8). The observed speedup gain from p=3 to p=4 was `3.458 - 2.737 = 0.721`. From p=4 to p=5 it was `3.640 - 3.458 = 0.182`: a **74.8% drop in marginal gain**. This is consistent with a transition from performance-oriented to efficiency-oriented core resources, but OS scheduling/other effects were not directly measured.

### Analytical roofline mapping

Use the measured simplified bound `Prooftop(I) = min(Ppython, BW * I)`. The assignment's analytical intensities are `Inaive≈0.125 FLOP/Byte` and `Itiled(BS*)≈BS*/12=8.0 FLOPs/Byte`. The reordered variant's true effective intensity was not independently measured, so using 0.125 for its comparison is a conservative *proxy*, not a measured access count. Because even `BW * 0.125 ≈ 2.884 GFLOP/s` exceeds the measured Python arithmetic ceiling (0.656 GFLOP/s), all three variants are limited by that lower empirical ceiling in this simplified comparison.

| Variant | Intensity used (FLOPs/Byte) | Simple roofline bound (GFLOP/s) | Observed GFLOP/s | Bound attained |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Naive | 0.125 | 0.655878 | 0.128888 | 19.65% |
| Reordered | 0.125 (proxy) | 0.655878 | 0.136722 | 20.85% |
| Blocked BS=96 | 8.0 | 0.655878 | 0.124816 | 19.03% |

The model should not be interpreted as an exact processor roofline because the 16-scalar Python arithmetic microbenchmark and GEMM implement different workloads and overhead profiles.

## Phase 4 — Skewed Workload Scheduling

Each item i ran `K(i) = 1 + floor((i/60)^2)` heavy trigonometric iterations. The workload covered N=12,000 items and p=8 processes. Per-process active times were recorded to calculate `λ = max(tworker) / mean(tworker)`.

### Table 4 — Wall time and imbalance

| Strategy | Wall time (ms) | λ | Result checksum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Static block | 4707.611 | 2.4768 | 2772.727602 |
| Cyclic | 2777.929 | 1.0253 | 2772.727602 |
| Dynamic C=1 | 2846.855 | 1.0016 | 2772.727602 |
| Dynamic C=4 | 2878.742 | 1.0076 | 2772.727602 |
| Dynamic C=16 | 2933.163 | 1.0138 | 2772.727602 |
| Dynamic C=64 | 3028.339 | 1.0347 | 2772.727602 |
| Dynamic C=256 | 3195.568 | 1.1332 | 2772.727602 |
| Dynamic C=1024 | 3981.566 | 1.6047 | 2772.727602 |

The large λ=2.4768 for static blocks reflects the concentration of expensive indices on later workers: the final worker was active 4611.76 ms, versus only 35.27 ms for the first. Cyclic assignment interleaves high- and low-cost iterations, achieving λ=1.0253 and the **fastest overall wall time**, 2777.929 ms.

Of the dynamic variants, **C*=1** gave the fastest wall time (2846.855 ms) and the most balanced measured worker durations (λ=1.0016), with speedup `4707.611 / 2846.855 = 1.654x` versus static. The C=1 counter is acquired for every item and adds synchronization overhead, so slightly slower wall time than cyclic is plausible even when λ is better. Larger C reduces chunk-claim frequency but concentrates the heavy tail in fewer workers: for C=1024, λ increased to 1.6047 and wall time to 3981.566 ms. The experiment supports small dynamic chunks for this skew pattern, but one wall-time sample per setting does not establish statistical reliability.

**Correctness:** All strategy checksums agree at 2772.727602.

## Experiment limitations and reproducibility

1. Phases 2 and 3 used a small count of timed trials; the compute-ceiling samples in Phase 3A showed substantial run-to-run variability.
2. Phase 1 does not show the expected false-sharing cutoff, so the cache-line size is based on hardware telemetry rather than a clear empirical time discontinuity.
3. Apple M2 mixes performance and efficiency cores; exact OS placement and frequency/thermal behavior were not recorded.
4. Phase 3B emitted cleanup warnings for exported shared-memory views; this should be fixed if the scripts are reused.
5. Roofline arithmetic values are Python-kernel throughputs and should not be represented as theoretical hardware FMA ceilings.

### `harness.py`

```python
from dataclasses import dataclass
import multiprocessing as mp
import time
from typing import Callable

@dataclass
class BenchStats:
    min_ms: float
    median_ms: float
    iqr_ms: float
    max_ms: float

def fnv1a_hash(s: str) -> int:
    h = 14695981039346656037
    for b in s.encode("utf-8"):
        h ^= b
        h = (h * 1099511628211) & 0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF
    return h

def run_benchmark(fn: Callable[[], None],
                  warmup_runs: int = 3,
                  timed_runs: int = 9) -> BenchStats:
    for _ in range(warmup_runs):
        fn()

    samples = []
    for _ in range(timed_runs):
        t0 = time.perf_counter_ns()
        fn()
        t1 = time.perf_counter_ns()
        samples.append((t1 - t0) / 1e6)

    samples.sort()
    n = len(samples)
    mid = n // 2
    med = samples[mid] if n % 2 else (samples[mid-1] + samples[mid]) / 2

    return BenchStats(
        samples[0],
        med,
        samples[(3*n)//4] - samples[n//4],
        samples[-1]
    )

def emit_verify_tag(task_id: str, student_seed: int,
                    primary_metric: float,
                    secondary_metric: float) -> str:
    hw = mp.cpu_count()
    payload = (
        f"{task_id}|{student_seed}|{hw}|"
        f"{primary_metric:.4f}|{secondary_metric:.4f}"
    )
    sig = fnv1a_hash(payload)
    return f"VERIFY_HASH[{task_id}]: {sig:016X} (P_log={hw})"
```

```python
        end = start + size
        chunks.append((start, end))
        start = end

    return chunks
```

```python
                    abs(c_view[i] - reference[i])
                    for i in range(N * N)
                )

                del c_view

                working_set_kb = (
                    3 * bs * bs * 8 / 1024
                )

                print(
                    f"{bs:3d} | "
                    f"{median_ms:10.3f} | "
                    f"{gflops:8.6f} | "
                    f"{working_set_kb:15.3f} | "
                    f"{max_error:.3e}",
                    flush=True
                )

                if max_error >= 1e-9:
                    raise RuntimeError(
                        f"Numerical mismatch for BS={bs}"
                    )

                results.append((bs, median_ms, gflops))

            best = max(results, key=lambda x: x[2])
            best_bs, best_ms, best_gflops = best

            print("\nFINAL GEMM ANALYSIS", flush=True)
            print(f"Best tile size: {best_bs}")
            print(f"Best time: {best_ms:.3f} ms")
            print(f"Best performance: {best_gflops:.6f} GFLOP/s")

            print(
                f"Naive to Reordered speedup: "
                f"{naive_ms / reordered_ms:.3f}x"
            )

            print(
                f"Naive to Best Blocked speedup: "
                f"{naive_ms / best_ms:.3f}x"
            )

            print(
                f"Best tile working set: "
                f"{3 * best_bs ** 2 * 8 / 1024:.3f} KB"
            )

            print(
                f"Naive operational intensity: "
                f"{0.125:.6f} FLOPs/Byte"
            )

            print(
                f"Tiled operational intensity: "
                f"{best_bs / 12:.6f} FLOPs/Byte"
            )

            print("\nPHASE 3 VERIFICATION", flush=True)
            print(
                emit_verify_tag(
                    "PHASE3",
                    STUDENT_ID,
                    naive_gflops,
                    best_gflops
                ),
                flush=True
            )

            print("\nPHASE 3 COMPLETED", flush=True)

    finally:
        for shm in blocks:
            shm.close()
            shm.unlink()


if __name__ == "__main__":
    mp.freeze_support()
    main()
```

```python
import math
import multiprocessing as mp
import time

from harness import emit_verify_tag

STUDENT_ID = 230103316
N = 12_000
P_PHYS = 8
CHUNK_SIZES = [1, 4, 16, 64, 256, 1024]


def compute_item(i):
    # K(i) = 1 + floor((i / 60)^2)
    iterations = 1 + (i * i) // 3600
    x = 0.01 + (i % 100) * 0.0001

    for k in range(iterations):
        x = (
            math.sin(x)
            + math.cos(x)
            + math.sqrt(abs(x) + 1.0)
        ) * 0.1

    return x


def worker(t, p, strategy, chunk_size,
           counter, lock, timings, results,
           start_event):

    start_event.wait()
    t0 = time.perf_counter_ns()
    total = 0.0

    if strategy == "block":
        start = t * N // p
        end = (t + 1) * N // p

        for i in range(start, end):
            total += compute_item(i)

    elif strategy == "cyclic":
        for i in range(t, N, p):
            total += compute_item(i)

    elif strategy == "dynamic":
        while True:
            with lock:
                start = counter.value
                counter.value += chunk_size

            if start >= N:
                break

            end = min(start + chunk_size, N)

            for i in range(start, end):
                total += compute_item(i)

    else:
        raise ValueError("Unknown scheduling strategy")

    t1 = time.perf_counter_ns()

    timings[t] = (t1 - t0) / 1e6
    results[t] = total


def run_experiment(strategy, chunk_size=0):
    ctx = mp.get_context("spawn")

    counter = ctx.Value("i", 0, lock=False)
    lock = ctx.Lock()

    timings = ctx.Array(
        "d", P_PHYS, lock=False
    )
    results = ctx.Array(
        "d", P_PHYS, lock=False
    )

    start_event = ctx.Event()

    processes = []

    for t in range(P_PHYS):
        process = ctx.Process(
            target=worker,
            args=(
                t,
                P_PHYS,
                strategy,
                chunk_size,
                counter,
                lock,
                timings,
                results,
                start_event
            )
        )
        processes.append(process)

    for process in processes:
        process.start()

    t0 = time.perf_counter_ns()
    start_event.set()

    for process in processes:
        process.join()

    t1 = time.perf_counter_ns()

    for process in processes:
        if process.exitcode != 0:
            raise RuntimeError(
                f"Worker failed: {process.exitcode}"
            )

    wall_ms = (t1 - t0) / 1e6

    worker_times = list(timings)
    mean_worker = sum(worker_times) / P_PHYS
    max_worker = max(worker_times)

    load_imbalance = max_worker / mean_worker
    checksum = sum(results)

    return {
        "wall_ms": wall_ms,
        "lambda": load_imbalance,
        "worker_times": worker_times,
        "checksum": checksum
    }


def main():
    print("=" * 70, flush=True)
    print("PHASE 4 — SKEWED WORKLOAD SCHEDULING", flush=True)
    print("=" * 70, flush=True)

    print(f"Student ID: {STUDENT_ID}")
    print("CPU: Apple M2")
    print(f"Physical cores: {P_PHYS}")
    print(f"Outer items: {N:,}")
    print("Workload: 1 + floor((i/60)^2)")

    experiments = [
        ("Static Block", "block", 0),
        ("Cyclic", "cyclic", 0)
    ]

    for chunk in CHUNK_SIZES:
        experiments.append(
            (f"Dynamic C={chunk}", "dynamic", chunk)
        )

    all_results = []

    print("\nRUNNING EXPERIMENTS", flush=True)

    for name, strategy, chunk in experiments:
        print(f"\nRunning {name}...", flush=True)

        result = run_experiment(strategy, chunk)

        all_results.append({
            "name": name,
            "strategy": strategy,
            "chunk": chunk,
            **result
        })

        print(
            f"Wall time: {result['wall_ms']:.3f} ms",
            flush=True
        )
        print(
            f"Load imbalance lambda: "
            f"{result['lambda']:.4f}",
            flush=True
        )
        print(
            f"Checksum: {result['checksum']:.6f}",
            flush=True
        )

    print("\nTABLE 4 — SCHEDULING RESULTS", flush=True)
    print(
        "Strategy | Wall(ms) | Lambda | Checksum",
        flush=True
    )

    for result in all_results:
        print(
            f"{result['name']:16s} | "
            f"{result['wall_ms']:10.3f} | "
            f"{result['lambda']:7.4f} | "
            f"{result['checksum']:.6f}",
            flush=True
        )

    print("\nINDIVIDUAL WORKER TIMES (ms)", flush=True)

    for result in all_results:
        formatted = ", ".join(
            f"{v:.2f}"
            for v in result["worker_times"]
        )
        print(
            f"{result['name']}: [{formatted}]",
            flush=True
        )

    reference = all_results[0]["checksum"]

    for result in all_results:
        if not math.isclose(
            result["checksum"],
            reference,
            rel_tol=1e-9,
            abs_tol=1e-6
        ):
            raise RuntimeError(
                f"Checksum mismatch: {result['name']}"
            )

    dynamic_results = [
        r for r in all_results
        if r["strategy"] == "dynamic"
    ]

    best = min(
        dynamic_results,
        key=lambda r: r["wall_ms"]
    )

    static = all_results[0]
    cyclic = all_results[1]

    print("\nFINAL SCHEDULING ANALYSIS", flush=True)
    print(f"Best dynamic chunk: {best['chunk']}")
    print(
        f"Best dynamic wall time: "
        f"{best['wall_ms']:.3f} ms"
    )
    print(
        f"Static wall time: "
        f"{static['wall_ms']:.3f} ms"
    )
    print(
        f"Cyclic wall time: "
        f"{cyclic['wall_ms']:.3f} ms"
    )
    print(
        f"Static / Dynamic speedup: "
        f"{static['wall_ms'] / best['wall_ms']:.3f}x"
    )

    print("\nPHASE 4 VERIFICATION", flush=True)
    print(
        emit_verify_tag(
            "PHASE4",
            STUDENT_ID,
            static["wall_ms"],
            best["wall_ms"]
        ),
        flush=True
    )

    print("\nPHASE 4 COMPLETED", flush=True)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    mp.freeze_support()
    main()
```
